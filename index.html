From a single wooden peel and a backyard brick oven to a custom-built kitchen on wheels, we bring authentic, wood-fired flavor straight to your neighborhood.

The Peel on Wheels started with a simple mission: to take the unmistakable taste of real, blistered, wood-fired pizza out of the traditional sit-down restaurant and put it on the open road. What began as weekend pop-up bakes for friends and local markets quickly grew into a full-fledged mobile pizzeria powered by a custom-installed, high-heat brick oven. Every pie is hand-stretched to order using 48-hour cold-fermented dough, crushed San Marzano tomatoes, and fresh, locally sourced toppings—sliding straight off our signature wooden peel and into your hands in under two minutes. Whether we are parked at a local brewery, a neighborhood festival, or catering a private event, The Peel on Wheels is all about hot crusts, melted cheese, and bringing people together around great food.
